      Our philosophy

      Three ideas behind every modern outfit

      I

      Proportion

      Balance a loose piece with a fitted one: wide trousers with a neat top, an oversized sweater with slim jeans.

      II

      Restraint

      Let one element lead, whether it’s a colour, a print or a statement shoe, and keep the rest quiet.

      III

      Texture

      When colours are simple, texture adds depth: knit, linen, leather, denim and wool all catch the light differently.

      Questions

      Style questions, answered

      What makes an outfit look modern?

      Modern outfits tend to have clean lines, a considered fit and a restrained colour palette, with one interesting detail such as an unexpected texture, proportion or accessory. Fit and simplicity matter more than following every trend.

      How many colours should I wear at once?

      A good starting point is the rule of three: up to three colours in one outfit, with neutrals such as black, white, grey, navy and beige counting as a backdrop. Let one colour lead and use the others to support it.

      Can I mix patterns?

      Yes. The easiest way is to vary the scale, such as a bold stripe with a small polka dot, and keep at least one colour shared between them. Start with one pattern plus a texture before trying two patterns.

      What does smart casual actually mean?

      Smart casual sits between office wear and weekend clothes: think chinos or dark jeans with a shirt, fine knit or blouse, an optional blazer and clean leather shoes or minimal trainers. No ripped denim, sportswear or flip-flops.

      How do I find my personal style?

      Look at the pieces you wear most and feel best in, save outfit pictures you love, and notice the patterns: colours, shapes and fabrics that keep appearing. Build around those rather than chasing every new trend.
