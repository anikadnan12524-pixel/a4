The Modern Outfits lookbook

Six modern outfits broken down piece by piece, with a styling tip for each. Every look can be recreated with wardrobe basics.

Spring

The off-duty trench

  • TopFine-knit crew-neck or a crisp white shirt
  • BottomStraight-leg jeans in a mid or light wash
  • LayerA classic trench, belted or left open
  • ShoesWhite leather trainers or loafers

Push the sleeves up slightly and let the shirt cuff show for an easy, polished finish.

All year

Head-to-toe black, softened

  • TopBlack knit or fitted tee
  • BottomBlack wide-leg trousers
  • LayerA camel or grey coat
  • ShoesBlack ankle boots or minimal sandals

Mix textures, such as ribbed knit, wool and leather, so an all-black outfit never looks flat.

Autumn

The weekend knit

  • TopChunky cream sweater
  • BottomDark chinos or straight jeans
  • LayerA chore jacket for cooler mornings
  • ShoesSuede desert boots or clean trainers

A light, textured knit against dark trousers is one of the easiest ways to look put-together off duty.

Summer

White shirt, black shorts

  • TopOversized white cotton or linen shirt
  • BottomTailored black shorts
  • LayerNone needed; carry a light cardigan
  • ShoesFlat leather sandals

Half-tuck the shirt at the front to define the waist without losing the relaxed shape.

Evening

The turtleneck dress

  • DressFitted black turtleneck midi dress
  • LayerA long tailored coat
  • AccentGold earrings or a slim belt
  • ShoesHeeled boots or pointed flats

Keep accessories simple and let the clean line of the dress do the work.

Workday

Smart city layers

  • TopFine merino roll-neck or an oxford shirt
  • BottomWool trousers in grey or navy
  • LayerAn unstructured blazer or overcoat
  • ShoesLeather derbies or minimal trainers

Swap a shirt and tie for a fine roll-neck under a blazer for a modern take on office dressing.

Make the looks your own

Swap colours using the colour matcher, check the dress code decoder before an event, and read our style guide for the principles behind each outfit.
